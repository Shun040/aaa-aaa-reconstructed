## The original work / 原作

*AAA-AAA*, 1978, by Marina Abramović and Ulay. The recording analysed for this work was
made and produced by R.T.B.F. in Liège in February 1978.

This repository does not contain the recording, any excerpt of its audio or video, or any
still from it. It contains:

- `score.json`, `analysis/phrases.json`, `analysis/intro_words.json`: timing, pitch and
  spectral measurements derived from analysing the recording;
- `intro_plan.json`: a brief quotation of the artists' spoken statement (about thirty
  words) with word timings, used to reconstruct the spoken introduction;
- `docs/comparison_archive_vs_reconstruction.png`: a spectrogram comparison produced for
  analysis and commentary.
